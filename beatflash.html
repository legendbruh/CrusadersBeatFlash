⚠️ What does Audio Calibration do?

Audio calibration compensates for timing differences between your device, headphones/speakers and the game.

0 ms = normal timing.
+100 ms = timing is shifted 0.1 seconds later.
-100 ms = timing is shifted 0.1 seconds earlier.

It does not change the song. It only changes when the game considers the beat to happen. If your taps feel consistently early or late, adjust it.
